**ROCKPILLAR TECHNOLOGY**

**Asclepios Growth & Marketing Operating System**

Research-to-Content Factory, Commerce Funnel, Multi-Channel Publishing & Learning Engine

Asclepios 增長及市場營運系統：由研究到內容、影片、網站、轉化及持續優化

Version 1.0 • 5 September 2026 • Internal Confidential

# Executive Summary / 執行摘要

- Growth is a dedicated operating department, not an afterthought added after the app is finished.

- Every approved research insight, product feature and customer question can become reusable marketing assets—but only through a classified, versioned production line rather than one giant master script.

- The content system should produce many different angles and formats from the same verified knowledge object: education, product explanation, short video, animation, blog, FAQ, social post, email and sales page.

- Asclepios Health is the primary commerce/product-information destination; Asclepios Sleep stays focused on sleep management and opens product education externally without losing app state.

- Content and campaigns are measured by actual outcomes. Performance data returns to the content, product and intelligence systems to improve future work.

| Growth principle: One verified knowledge base, many creative expressions, measurable customer journeys. |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

# 1. Growth Department Mission

- Educate users about sleep problems and practical solutions.

- Explain why Asclepios is different: research-backed sleep management + products + longitudinal personalisation.

- Convert appropriate interest into app usage, product purchase or programme participation without undermining trust.

- Build owned media assets before launch so marketing does not start from zero on launch day.

- Create a repeatable engine that can operate across website, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Xiaohongshu/Chinese channels, email and future paid advertising.

# 3. Content Production Line

1.  Ingest: approved research, product update, app feature, customer question or campaign brief.

2.  Classify: topic, scenario, audience, product, claim status, funnel stage and language.

3.  Generate concept variants: educational, myth-busting, demonstration, story, FAQ, comparison and conversion.

4.  Script: 15–30 sec, 45–60 sec, 2–5 min and long-form versions where appropriate.

5.  Creative: storyboard, animation direction, voice style, on-screen text and visuals.

6.  Review: scientific/safety/claim check; brand and UX consistency.

7.  Produce/store: asset ID, version, source references, channel-ready files.

8.  Publish/schedule when channels are available.

9.  Measure: watch time, click-through, app start, product view, checkout, conversion, returns/complaints.

10. Learn: winning angles are reused; weak claims/creative are adjusted; customer questions become new content.

# 5. Research-to-Marketing Translation Rules

- Never convert a mechanistic finding into a guaranteed product outcome.

- Use 'informed by', 'research-backed', 'evidence suggests', 'may support' only when the source and context justify the wording.

- Do not say Asclepios has clinically proven an outcome unless Asclepios itself has completed appropriate research.

- Named institutions may be referenced only when the cited work genuinely originates from or is affiliated with that institution and the wording does not imply endorsement.

- Every public research statement should be traceable to an internal evidence ID.

- Marketing can simplify language, but not change the scientific meaning.

# 11. Voice & Localisation

- Create an approved voice library: calm adult female, calm adult male and selected warm personas; keep childlike voices optional and context-appropriate.

- Scripts should be locale-adapted rather than literal translation.

- English, zh-HK, zh-TW and zh-CN assets should share the same evidence/claim object but can differ in examples and tone.

- Voice content inside the app should remain optional and not become annoying or infantilising.

# 13. Marketing Automation — Target State

- When a new approved research insight/product feature is added, automatically create content briefs rather than waiting for manual requests.

- Generate a queue of variants instead of publishing every generated draft.

- Human/AI review gates protect scientific accuracy, claims, brand and platform policy.

- Once channels are connected, scheduling/publishing can be automated with exception handling.

- Campaign and conversion data should return to the Growth learning engine to propose the next tests.

# 14. Customer Service ↔ Growth Loop

- Repeated CS question → FAQ/article/video candidate.

- High abandonment on a product page → content/UX experiment.

- Misuse or misunderstanding → how-to asset and in-app guidance update.

- Refund reason → expectation-setting and eligibility review.

- Positive user outcome story → consented case study candidate; never fabricate testimonials.

# 15. Referral, Affiliate, Agent & Discount Architecture

- Every referral/agent/affiliate relationship needs a unique attribution code/link.

- Separate customer discount from referrer/agent commission logic.

- Define eligibility, commission basis, refund clawback, payout timing and fraud controls.

- Attribution should survive from content click → website → checkout → account where legally/technically appropriate.

- The system should support future B2B partners without hardcoding one commission model.

# 19. Definition of Done for Growth OS V1

- Content is categorised and traceable to source knowledge, not stored as an unstructured pile.

- At least one product page template is working end-to-end.

- Research page exists and source links are verifiable.

- Initial content backlog has multi-format scripts and clear statuses.

- The app links to product/research pages without losing user state.

- Conversion events are defined from content through purchase/activation.

- A clear review/publishing workflow exists.

- Performance data can be fed back into the next content and product decisions.
