# Company / Brand / Product Architecture

Status: ACTIVE WORKING ARCHITECTURE
Owner: Edmund Yeung
Date: 2026-09-07

## 1. Why this exists
This document prevents company, brand and product identities from being mixed together during product development, website work, marketing, app naming, reporting or future company formation.

The original Asclepios Sleep work began from a sleep-app build request. That does NOT mean the whole company is a sleep-app company.

## 2. Current strategic hierarchy

### Level 1 — Group / parent direction
**RockPillar Medical**

Working parent/group direction for the medical-health business. Final legal/corporate structure is not yet fixed.

RockPillar Medical may sit above or be structurally related to Asclepios Health. Do not invent a final legal ownership chart before Edmund approves it.

### Level 2 — Health platform / operating brand
**Asclepios Health**

Asclepios Health is the broader health platform / business brand, not the name of one sleep app.

Potential long-term scope includes:
- multiple health applications;
- physical health products;
- sleep, gut, weight, mood, exercise or other health-management products/services;
- future physical retail;
- future clinic / therapeutic / professional-care services where legally and commercially appropriate;
- shared customer, product, education, commerce and health-platform infrastructure.

Asclepios Health should be designed as a platform that can continuously launch additional products and services, similar to a studio/platform company that can create multiple apps/products over time.

### Level 3 — Individual products / apps / services
The current sleep-management product is ONE child product under Asclepios Health.

Its final independent product/app name is **NOT YET CONFIRMED**.

Therefore:
- do not use `Asclepios` alone as if it is the final sleep-app product name;
- do not make Asclepios Health visually or commercially equivalent to the sleep-management app;
- do not lock the company website, app icon, PWA name, product copy or future architecture to the assumption that Asclepios Health only does sleep;
- the sleep product should eventually have its own distinct product identity, app icon and product name while retaining an endorsed relationship to Asclepios Health.

Working naming relationship may later follow a pattern such as:
`[Sleep Product Name] by Asclepios Health`
or another owner-approved endorsement system.

This is only a structural example, not a naming decision.

## 3. Current sleep product status
The current sleep-management system remains the active first major product build.

Its functional scope includes:
- personalised sleep management;
- adaptive assessment;
- Tonight / Day / Evening / Pre-Sleep flows;
- programmes;
- sleep education;
- music / environment;
- product-use guidance and adherence;
- reports / progress;
- research-backed Sleep Intelligence;
- optional AI where it materially improves the experience.

This scope does not make it the company itself.

## 4. Website / commerce architecture principle
The future public Asclepios Health website should represent the broader Asclepios Health platform and business.

It may contain:
- company / platform story;
- physical products;
- future product/service categories;
- commerce;
- customer support;
- member access;
- entry points into individual digital products/apps, including the sleep-management product.

The website may be built first on Vercel staging before a final public domain is purchased or attached.

The absence of a live `AsclepiosHealth.com` domain does not block staging implementation.

## 5. App architecture principle
Individual apps/products must have their own product identity and should not automatically inherit the Asclepios Health corporate/platform identity as their final consumer-facing app identity.

The sleep-management app should eventually have:
- its own final name;
- its own simplified app icon;
- its own product-level visual identity;
- clear endorsement/relationship to Asclepios Health;
- integration with Asclepios Health account, commerce, products and future shared infrastructure where appropriate.

## 6. Brand and icon rule
Corporate/platform logo and app icon are separate design problems.

**Asclepios Health corporate/platform mark**
- may be more formal;
- may appear on website, packaging, documents, clinics/retail, corporate material.

**Sleep product app icon**
- should be simple at small mobile size;
- strong silhouette;
- minimal detail;
- modern consumer-health feel;
- not a miniature realistic corporate crest;
- final design only after the sleep product naming direction is confirmed.

Any interim icon is temporary and must not be treated as the final brand architecture.
