## 55-second explainer
### Script
“You may have seen mouth taping promoted as a shortcut to better sleep. The evidence is much less simple than social media makes it sound.

At Asclepios, we don’t position Sleep Tape as a treatment for snoring, sleep apnoea or nasal blockage. Its role is smaller and clearer: for suitable adults who can breathe comfortably through the nose, it can provide a gentle physical cue to help keep the lips together as part of a bedtime routine.

Suitability comes first. Don’t use it when your nose is blocked or nasal breathing is uncomfortable. If you wake gasping, feel air hunger, have persistent loud snoring with pauses, or suspect sleep apnoea, that needs proper attention rather than stronger taping.

And if the tape causes breathing restriction, anxiety or significant skin irritation, remove it and stop.

The Asclepios idea is simple: use the physical product where it fits, but connect it to education and the wider pattern behind your sleep. Use a cue, not a cure.”

## Founder/product 2–3 minute outline
This is an outline, not a verbatim medical lecture.

1. **Why we made it** — customers want simple physical sleep tools, but the market often overstates what they do.
2. **What it actually is** — a gentle behavioural cue for suitable nasal breathers.
3. **What it is not** — not an OSA/snoring treatment, not a CPAP replacement, not a guarantee of better sleep or oxygen.
4. **Suitability before selling** — comfortable nasal breathing, clear nose, ability to remove independently; safety information visible before purchase/use.
5. **How it fits Asclepios** — physical product + education + Sleep Intelligence/routine support; the product is one intervention, not the whole answer.
6. **What we want the customer to learn** — notice patterns, use the smallest useful intervention, stop if uncomfortable, escalate concerning breathing symptoms appropriately.
7. **Close** — “Use a cue, not a cure.”

## Five FAQ/social-response snippets
1. **Will it stop snoring?** We don’t promise that. Snoring has multiple causes and can sometimes be a sign of OSA; Sleep Tape is not a treatment for it.
2. **What if my nose is blocked?** Don’t use it while nasal breathing is uncomfortable.
3. **Can I replace CPAP with it?** No. Never replace prescribed OSA treatment with mouth tape.
4. **What if I feel I can’t breathe?** Remove it immediately and don’t push through breathing restriction.
5. **Is it proven to improve sleep?** Evidence on mouth taping remains limited and mixed, so we position it as a behavioural routine accessory rather than a guaranteed sleep treatment.
